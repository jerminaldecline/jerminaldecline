    How to read this
    • Stock is read once a day. On Amazon it is the quantity the listing will let a cart take, which is Amazon's sellable count for that listing. On the Shopify store it is the exact count the cart reports, which stops at 80, so 80 means "at least 80".
    • Units sold is any fall in stock between two readings. The data cannot tell a retail sale from stock being moved to another channel or a wholesale order, so large single-day falls (40 or more units and at least half the stock) are shown separately as bulk drops and left out of sales unless you tick the box.
    • Revenue is units multiplied by the listing's usual in-stock price. Net is that figure multiplied by whatever margin you set above. Neither is a real figure from the business.
    • Restocks and sales inside the same day cancel out in a single reading, so both are undercounts. Days with no usable reading are skipped, and a fall across a gap is booked on the day the next reading landed.
    • Amazon lists each coffee once per size with the same name. Size is inferred from the usual price (5 lb bags around $85). About a third of Amazon listings show the 5 lb price while the 12 oz bag is out of stock; those reads are kept in the price history but do not affect valuations.
